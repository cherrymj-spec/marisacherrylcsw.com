The practice

The Autonomic Regulation Protocol

A structured 14 session program built around HRV biofeedback, delivered in my private practice to clients in California and Florida. Sessions pair resonance frequency breathing with a chest strap measurement, so there is an objective reading to look at together, session after session, instead of self-report alone. The protocol is preregistered for a pilot study on the Open Science Framework. Preliminary, and said plainly: the pilot has no outcome data yet.
